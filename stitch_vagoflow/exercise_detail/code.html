Exercise

Cold Water Facial Immersion

Diving Reflex

2 Minutes

psychology

How it Works

This exercise stimulates the vagus nerve through the diving reflex, a natural physiological response to cold water. This helps lower your heart rate and promote a state of deep calm.

Instructions

  • 1

    Fill a bowl with cold water. Add ice cubes for a more potent effect, but start with cool water if you're new to this.

  • 2

    Take a deep breath and hold it. Lean forward and gently submerge your face in the water, focusing on the area around your eyes and cheeks.

  • 3

    Keep your face submerged for 15-30 seconds. You can come up for air and repeat 2-3 times as comfortable.

  • 4

    After the final immersion, gently pat your face dry and take a few moments to notice the shift in your body and mind.

warning

Safety First

Consult your doctor before trying this exercise if you have a heart condition or low blood pressure. Do not force yourself to hold your breath.
